This tool was developed as a practical interim measure to bridge the gap between the capabilities envisaged for the CDP and what is currently available. It is designed to support procurement planning by providing broad, high-level intelligence across service categories, suppliers, and framework agreements.

Users should be mindful of its limitations. Reports are generated by aggregating data from three distinct notice types across two procurement regimes, and the underlying notices may themselves contain inaccuracies, inconsistencies, or incomplete information. Outputs should not be relied upon for detailed or specific research, or for high-stakes decision-making, without independent verification.

Always refer to the original notices published on Find a Tender or Contracts Finder to confirm that information is accurate and current.

Data sources: This tool draws exclusively on PA23 Contract Details (UK7), PCR 2015 Contract Award (F03), and Contracts Finder Awarded Opportunity notices. It does not include pre-market engagement notices, procurement pipelines, Prior Information Notices (PINs), or any other notice types.
